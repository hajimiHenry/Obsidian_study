## 一、实用短语 Practical Phrases

### Group 1

| 短语 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| major in | 主修（某一）科目 |
| in succession | 连续发生；接连发生 |
| be bound to | 肯定会，注定 |
| stand up for | 支持，维护 |
| speculate about/on | 推测，猜测 |
| invest sb./sth. with sth. | 赋予（某人/某物）以某种性质 |
| be liable to do sth. | 可能/易于做某事 |

#### 例句

1. **major in** — 主修（某一）科目
   - 中文：对具有电脑知识人才的高需求是我选择在大学主修计算机科学的原因。
   - 英文：The high demand for persons with knowledge about computers is why I chose to major in computer science at the university.

2. **in succession** — 连续发生
   - 中文：她是一个颇有成就的运动员，连续四次获得冠军。
   - 英文：She is an accomplished athlete and won the championship four times in succession.

3. **be bound to** — 肯定会
   - 中文：只要我们尽力而为，不管结果是输是赢，我们必定可以从这次经历中有所收获。
   - 英文：As long as we try our best, we are bound to gain something positive from the experience, whether we win or lose.

4. **stand up for** — 支持，维护
   - 中文：老板在会议上支持了我，使我免受了一些尴尬。
   - 英文：The boss stood up for me at the meeting, sparing me some embarrassment.

5. **speculate about/on** — 猜测，推测
   - 中文：要推测工会和资方之间的谈判结果，还为时过早。
   - 英文：It is too early to speculate about the outcome of the negotiations between the worker's union and the company's leadership.

6. **invest sb./sth. with sth.** — 赋予（某人或某物）以（某种性质）
   - 中文：学习人文学科赋予你批判思维的能力，为你提供无限的机遇。
   - 英文：Studying humanities invests you with critical thinking and provides you with the scope of many opportunities.

7. **be liable to do sth.** — 可能/易于做某事
   - 中文：如果现在你不能勇于面对自己的错误，那么将来有可能会重蹈覆辙。
   - 英文：If you can't face up to your mistake now, you are liable to repeat it in the future.

### Group 2

| 短语 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| separate from | 把……分开；分隔 |
| take a break from | 中断；暂停 |
| tend to do sth. | 倾向于；往往会；易于做某事 |
| base upon/on sth. | 以……为基础；以……为根据 |
| saddle sb. with sth. | 使承担苦差事；使负重担 |
| give way to sth. | 被代替 |
| back off | 放弃；退出 |
| wonder at | 对某事感到惊讶，感到诧异 |
| in company with | 与……一起 |

#### 例句

1. **separate from** — 把……分开；分隔
   - 中文：商业的银行帐户需跟个人的银行帐户分开。
   - 英文：Business bank accounts need to be separated from personal ones.

2. **take a break from** — 中断；暂停
   - 中文：她暂停了一会儿工作，给自己泡了一点咖啡，然后几分钟后继续工作。
   - 英文：She took a break from her work to make herself some coffee and then resumed her work a few minutes later.

3. **tend to do sth.** — 倾向于；往往会
   - 中文：人们随着年纪的增长往往不需要那么多睡眠。
   - 英文：People tend to need less sleep as they get older.

4. **base upon/on sth.** — 以……为基础；以……为根据
   - 中文：这些是成为一名成功的演说家的关键因素，是以我自己的经验为基础的。
   - 英文：These are the key factors for being a successful speaker, and they are based on my own experience.

5. **saddle sb. with sth.** — 使承担苦差事；使负重担
   - 中文：由于生产成本的提高，造成了该公司的负债。
   - 英文：The company is saddled with debt due to the consequence of an increased cost of production.

6. **give way to sth.** — 被代替
   - 中文：他对老板不给他加薪的决定感到非常失望，很快他的失望变成了愤怒。
   - 英文：He was greatly disappointed over his boss' decision against giving him a pay raise, and soon his disappointment gave way to anger.

7. **back off** — 放弃；退出
   - 中文：当杰瑞意识到需多少工作量才能成功地完成这个项目时，他放弃了。
   - 英文：Jerry backed off the project when he realized how much work it would take to successfully complete the project.

8. **wonder at** — 对某事感到惊讶，感到诧异
   - 中文：去山里观光的游客对这个地区天气变化之快感到诧异。
   - 英文：Visitors to mountainous areas tend to wonder at the speed with which the weather can change in such areas.

9. **in company with** — 与……一起
   - 中文：与石油市场一起，美元在1986年经历了一个艰难的时期。
   - 英文：In company with the oil market, the US dollar went through a bad phase in 1986.

## 二、段落翻译

### 1. 数字化教育

**中文原文：**

近年来，随着互联网技术的发展，我国的数字化教育资源建设取得了巨大的成就。很多高校建立了自己的数字化学习平台，数字化教学在教育中发挥着越来越大的作用。和传统教学方式相比，数字化教学方式有很大的优势。一方面，数字化教学使教学资源得以全球共享；另一方面，它拓展了学习者的学习时间和空间，人们可以随时随地通过互联网进入数字化的虚拟学校学习。这使得人类从接受一次性教育走向终身学习成为可能。

**英文译文：**

In recent years, with the development of Internet technology, the construction of digital education resources of our country has made great achievements. Many universities have set up their own digital learning platforms, and digital teaching is playing an increasingly important role in education. Compared with the traditional way of teaching, the digital way has a lot of advantages. On the one hand, digital teaching makes global sharing of teaching resources possible; on the other hand, it expands the learner's study time and space to learn, allowing people to get access to the digital virtual schools through the Internet anytime and anywhere. These advantages make it possible for people to shift from one-time learning to lifelong learning.

### 2. 英语专业毕业生就业

**中文原文：**

今年中国有699万高校毕业生进入就业市场，求职变得十分困难。对于仅凭借语言技能来找工作的英语专业毕业生来说，更是难上加难。由于学生整体的英语水平不断提高，英语专业毕业生正在失去他们的优势。虽然具备了良好的英文沟通能力，但他们通常缺乏其他专业知识。因此，专家称就求职而言，考取"双学位"（double major）是英语专业毕业生的一个理想选择。

**重点词汇：**
- 双学位：double major
- 求职：job hunting
- 语言技能：language skills
- 英语专业毕业生：English majors / graduates of English

### 3. 孝道

**中文原文：**

孝道（filial piety）是中国古代社会的基本道德规范（code of ethics）。中国人把孝视为人格之本、家庭和睦之本、国家安康之本。由于孝道是儒家伦理思想的核心，它成了中国社会千百年来维系家庭关系的道德准则。它毫无疑问是中华民族的一种传统美德。孝道文化是一个复合概念，内容丰富，涉及面广。它既有文化理念，又有制度礼仪（institutional etiquette）。一般来说，它指社会要求子女对父母应尽的义务，包括尊敬、关爱、赡养老人等等。孝道是古老的"东方文明"之根本。

**英文译文：**

Filial piety is the basic code of ethics in the ancient Chinese society. Chinese people consider filial piety as the essence of a person's integrity, family harmony, and the nation's well-being. With filial piety being the core of Confucian ethics, it has been the moral standard for the Chinese society to maintain the family relationship for thousands of years. It's undoubtedly a traditional Chinese virtue. The culture of filial piety is a complex concept, rich in content and wide in range. It includes not only cultural ideas but also institutional etiquettes. Generally speaking, it refers to the obligation of children to their parents required by the society, including respect, care, support for the elderly and so forth. Filial piety is fundamental to the ancient "Eastern civilization".
